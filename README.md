# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project built using **Microsoft Excel** to analyze pizza sales performance, customer ordering patterns, and product popularity.

The dashboard presents key performance indicators (KPIs), daily and hourly order trends, sales distribution by pizza category and size, and the best- and worst-selling pizzas. It helps transform raw sales data into meaningful insights for business decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall pizza sales performance through key business KPIs.
- Analyze daily and hourly ordering trends to identify peak periods.
- Compare sales contributions across pizza categories and sizes.
- Identify the top 5 and bottom 5 pizzas based on total quantity sold.
- Present sales insights through an interactive dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Pivot Tables and data aggregation
- Excel charts and data visualization
- KPI reporting
- Interactive date timeline and filtering

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Performance KPIs
A summary of total revenue, average order value, total pizzas sold, total orders, and average pizzas per order.

### 2. Daily Order Trends
A bar chart comparing order volumes across the days of the week to identify the busiest days.

### 3. Hourly Order Trends
A line chart showing order activity throughout the day to highlight peak ordering hours.

### 4. Sales by Pizza Category
A doughnut chart showing the percentage distribution of sales across Chicken, Classic, Supreme, and Veggie categories.

### 5. Sales by Pizza Size
A pie chart comparing sales contributions from Large, Medium, Regular, X-Large, and XX-Large pizzas.

### 6. Pizzas Sold by Category
A horizontal bar chart comparing total quantities sold across the four pizza categories.

### 7. Best- and Worst-Selling Pizzas
Two horizontal bar charts highlight the top 5 and bottom 5 pizzas based on total pizzas sold.

### 8. Interactive Date Filter
A date timeline allows users to filter the dashboard by order date and explore sales over different periods.

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- **Friday** records the highest daily order count, with 281 orders.
- **Classic** is the leading pizza category by quantity sold, with 1,178 pizzas.
- **Large** pizzas account for the largest share of sales by size, at 46.16%.
- **The Pepperoni Pizza** is the top-selling pizza by quantity, with 205 pizzas sold.
- **The Brie Carre Pizza** is the lowest-selling pizza in the displayed bottom-five ranking, with 44 pizzas sold.
- Order activity peaks around **12 PM and 5 PM**, with 210 and 216 orders respectively in the displayed hourly trend.

These patterns can help a business plan staffing, understand product preferences, and make more informed inventory and promotional decisions.

## 💼 Business Value

This dashboard demonstrates how spreadsheet-based analytics can support sales monitoring and operational planning. By combining KPIs, time-based trends, and product-level comparisons, it provides a clear overview of business performance.
